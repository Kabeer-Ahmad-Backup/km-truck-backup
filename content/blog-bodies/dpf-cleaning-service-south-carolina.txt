What Is a Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF)?

A diesel particulate filter, commonly known as a DPF, is an important emissions control component designed to capture soot and harmful particles produced during diesel engine combustion. This filtration system reduces environmental pollution and helps diesel vehicles comply with emission standards required for safe transportation operations. Over time, the filter becomes clogged with carbon deposits that restrict airflow and reduce engine efficiency(https://www.kmtrucktrailers.com/).
